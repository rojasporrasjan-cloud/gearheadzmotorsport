Shipping & Returns Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Shipping Policy

Where do we ship?
We currently ship exclusively within the United States. International shipping is not available at this time.

Processing & Delivery Times:
All our gear is printed to order. Orders are typically processed and printed within 2-3 business days. Standard shipping within the US generally takes an additional 5-7 business days.

Shipping Costs:
Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on the standard US rates.

Returns & Exchanges

All Sales Final:
Due to the limited and print-on-demand nature of our drops, all sales are final. We do not offer returns or refunds for buyer's remorse.

Exchanges for Sizing or Defects:
If you receive a defective item or need to exchange for a different size, please contact us within 14 days of delivery. The item must be unworn and in its original condition. Contact us on Instagram @2gearheadz to initiate an exchange.
