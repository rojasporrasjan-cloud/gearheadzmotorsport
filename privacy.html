Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

GearHeadz Motorsports ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from gearheadzmotorsports.com (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including your web browser, IP address, and time zone. When you make a purchase, we collect your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information (processed securely via Stripe), email address, and phone number.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the Order Information to fulfill any orders placed through the Site (including processing your payment information, arranging for shipping, and providing invoices/order confirmations). We also use this information to communicate with you and screen orders for potential risk or fraud.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your Personal Information with third parties to help us use your Personal Information, as described above. For example, we use Stripe to process payments and our print-on-demand partners to fulfill your orders. We may also share your Information to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Your Rights

If you are a resident of the United States (including California), you have the right to access personal information we hold about you and to ask that it be corrected, updated, or deleted. Please contact us through our Instagram @2gearheadz to exercise these rights.

5. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, please contact us via Instagram at @2gearheadz.
